How the value is worked out

implied = 10.04 × TVL^0.80 × (1 + 1.5 × clamp(change_7d, −50, +60) / 100) × (1 + 0.45 × min(age_years, 5))

The constant is not picked by hand. It is anchored to the market: the comparables curve log10(mcap) = 3.908 + 0.405 × log10(TVL), fitted on the 94 trading protocols whose market cap is comparable to their TVL (R² 0.103). The constant is chosen so the model’s median, at neutral growth and age, equals that curve’s median prediction over this board. The curve was also tested blind: fitted on four fifths of the comparables and scored on the fifth (16 protocols), the median error was ×3.40 — wide, and stated as wide, which is why the curve anchors the scale and the power law carries the ranking.

Growth is linear and clamped, so one wild week cannot crown a protocol on its own. Age is the years since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol with no token recorded, capped at five. The board holds only genuine launch candidates: pre-token, and not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not an exchange, and not a versioned copy — the watchlist’s own rule, without its $50M floor, so early protocols below the floor are found too. Protocols whose token already trades are used only to fit the curve; they are not ranked here.

Verdict rule. FARM implied value at least $50M and 7-day TVL change at least −10%. THIN implied value at least $10M and change at least −30%. SKIP anything else: the farm costs more in time than it is likely to return.

Implied value is this entry’s own claim from the snapshot’s figures. It never predicts whether or when a token launches. A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none: its DefiLlama record may simply not carry one. Farm plans are a reader’s starting point, not financial advice; check the protocol yourself before you put money in.
